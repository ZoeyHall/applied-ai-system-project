## Prioritizing care tasks
Health and safety tasks come first: medication, feeding, water, and walks needed for a dog to relieve itself. Hygiene tasks such as litter box cleaning come next. Enrichment and grooming are important for long-term wellbeing but can move to another day when time is short.

## Choosing times for tasks
Pets do best with a predictable routine. Schedule feeding at the same times every day, and pair a walk or play session with a set part of the day, such as before work. Avoid putting two hands-on tasks for different pets at the same minute if only one person is available.

## Weekly and occasional tasks
Baths, nail trims, and deep cleaning of litter boxes or bedding are usually weekly or less often. Put them on a lighter day so they don't crowd out daily essentials.
