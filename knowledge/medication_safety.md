## Giving medication on schedule
Give medication at the same time each day, exactly as the veterinarian prescribed, and finish the full course even if the pet seems better. Medication tasks should be treated as high priority in a daily plan. If a dose is missed, ask the vet or pharmacist what to do instead of doubling the next dose.

## Human medicines are not safe for pets
Never give a pet human pain relievers such as acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen (Advil) unless a veterinarian specifically tells you to. Acetaminophen is especially dangerous for cats. Dosages for pets depend on weight, species, and health, so only a veterinarian should set a dose.

## Tips for pilling
Hiding a pill in a small treat or pill pocket works for many dogs. For cats, a pill pocket or a pet piller tool can help. Follow a pill with a small amount of water or food so it does not stick in the throat. Ask the vet whether a liquid or chewable form is available if pilling is hard.
