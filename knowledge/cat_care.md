## Litter box care
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully replace the litter and wash the box every 1 to 2 weeks. A common rule is one box per cat plus one extra. Put boxes in quiet spots away from food and water. A cat that suddenly stops using the box may be stressed or unwell and should see a vet.

## Feeding schedule
Most adult cats do well with two or more measured meals a day. Many cats prefer several small meals, which puzzle feeders can provide. Cats are obligate carnivores and need a complete cat food; dog food does not meet their needs. Keep fresh water available, and consider a fountain since many cats drink more from moving water.

## Play and enrichment
Indoor cats benefit from two or three short play sessions a day of about 10 to 15 minutes each. Wand toys and chase games that end with a "catch" satisfy hunting instincts. If you use a laser pointer, finish the session with a physical toy the cat can grab so the game doesn't end in frustration. Climbing spaces and window perches also help.

## Grooming
Short-haired cats usually need brushing about once a week; long-haired cats need brushing several times a week, ideally daily, to prevent mats and hairballs. Trim nails every 2 to 3 weeks if the cat does not wear them down on a scratching post.

## Plants and foods that are dangerous for cats
Lilies are extremely toxic to cats: even pollen or water from the vase can cause kidney failure. Also keep cats away from onions, garlic, chocolate, alcohol, and essential oils. Contact a veterinarian immediately if a cat is exposed.
