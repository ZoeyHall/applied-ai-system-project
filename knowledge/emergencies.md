## When to call a vet immediately
Contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital right away for difficulty breathing, seizures, collapse, heavy bleeding, suspected poisoning, a swollen hard belly (especially in large dogs), straining to urinate with little or no urine (especially in male cats), or repeated vomiting. Do not wait to see if it passes.

## Poison control
The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center can be reached at (888) 426-4435, 24 hours a day (a consultation fee may apply). Have the product packaging nearby when you call. Do not make a pet vomit unless a veterinarian tells you to.
